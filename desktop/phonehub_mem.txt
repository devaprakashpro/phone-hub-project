  1. Auto-connect on launch — the app remembers your laptop (host + pairing
  token) and silently re-links when you open it. Verified: connected in ~2s, 
  zero taps.
  2. Auto-reconnect with backoff — if the link drops (laptop sleeps, Wi-Fi blip,
  desktop restarts), the phone keeps retrying (2→4→8→16→30s) and re-links
  automatically. Verified: reconnected in ~1s after I killed and restarted the 
  desktop, zero taps, battery resumed.
  3. Persistent background link — a LinkService foreground service keeps the
  connection alive when the app is backgrounded, so
  notifications/battery/clipboard/files keep flowing.

  Found & fixed a real crash along the way

  The connectedDevice foreground-service type needs a second permission
  (CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE) — without it, Android threw a SecurityException that
  crashed the whole app. Fixed the permission and made the service fail-safe so
  an FGS error can never take the app down again.
